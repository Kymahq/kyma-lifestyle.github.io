User Data Deletion

Version 1.0 · Effective 2 November 2026

Who this page is for

If you have chatted on WhatsApp with an assistant powered by Kyma, this page explains how to ask for the personal data from those conversations to be deleted.

Kyma Intelligence Private Limited (“Kyma”, “we”) runs these assistants for financial institutions, such as banks, non-banking financial companies and insurers. The institution you were chatting with decides what data is collected and why. Kyma processes it on their behalf, as explained in our Privacy Policy.

How to request deletion

Email vatsal@kymahq.com with the subject “Data deletion request” and include:

  • The mobile number you used on WhatsApp
  • The name of the business you were chatting with, if you know it

You can also send your request directly to that business.

What happens next

  • We will reply within 7 working days to confirm we have received your request.
  • We will pass your request to the business you were chatting with without delay and help them carry it out.
  • Once the deletion is complete, we will confirm it to you by email.

What is deleted

Your conversation messages and the personal details you shared in them, such as your name, mobile number and any documents you sent, are deleted from Kyma's systems.

If the law requires some information to be kept for longer, for example under financial regulations, we will tell you what is kept and why.

Deleting your data from Kyma does not delete the chat from your own WhatsApp app. You can delete it there yourself.

Contact

Contact: Vatsal Kanakiya
Email: vatsal@kymahq.com

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
